ESP32-S3-Zero (N8R8) Firmware Build Notes
- Target Hardware: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero N8R8 (8MB Flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM) mounted on Seeed robot carrier.
- Multi-profile support configured: Robot Mode (4-leg kinematics), Claw Mode (MG90S), and Camera Profile.
- Servos mapped to hardware pins GPIO 1, 2, 3, 4 with 13-bit/14-bit 50Hz PWM timers.
- Servo lines actively clamped LOW on boot with hardware pull-downs to prevent power-on twitches.
- Audio Subsystem: HT517 I2S Class-D Mono Power Amplifier:
  * Dedicated to Controller 1 (I2S_NUM_1) so I2S_NUM_0 remains available for hardware PDM RX microphone.
  * Default Wiring Preset 1: Waveshare 
  Pin 10 (GPIO 10 BCLK) -> HT517 D10, 
  Pin 11 (GPIO 11 LRC) -> HT517 D11, 
  Pin 12 (GPIO 12 DIN) -> HT517 D12.
  * Alternative carrier pin presets supported at runtime via Web UI and REPL ('pins 8 7 9' or 'pins 12 11 10') and saved to NVS.
  * Dynamic clock & slot reconfiguration on playback with silence drain and auto-disable to prevent HT517 DC latchup, pop noises, and idle carrier hiss.
  * Built-in synthesized 880Hz/1760Hz dual-tone chime ('beep' command / '/beep' web route) for instant hardware line verification independent of WAV assets.
- Ultrasonic obstacle sensor mapped to  GPIO 6 (Echo). GPIO 5 (Trig) 
- Native USB Serial/JTAG console and Web/BLE provisioning active.
